About

Background & focus

I'm a Cloud Engineer based in Hosur, Tamil Nadu, currently working as Senior Associate Cloud Engineer at TVS Electronics, supporting TVS Motor Company. I started my career hands-on with IAM and RBAC access management in Azure and AWS, and have since grown into owning enterprise-scale cloud governance, hybrid networking, and Zero-Trust security across 50+ subscriptions and 250+ virtual machines.
